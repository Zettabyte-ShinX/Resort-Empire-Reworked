## Appendix B — Tips text

`serbi.Tips.listing` has 21 entries but only 20 unique keys: `upgradeBooth` appears twice. The duplicate is preserved below because it affects page count and “new tip” state.

| # | Key | Text |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `moveStage` | Click hand symbol and drag it on the screen. <br>You can also use A - S - W - D or arrow keys to scroll. |
| 2 | `clickOnObject` | Click on object to see it's properties. |
| 3 | `scenery1` | Plants can planted on a grass tile. |
| 4 | `clearedMission` | Clearing missions will give you additional money. |
| 5 | `clickNavigator1` | Click the blue tabs at the top to find plant, person or booth.<br>You can see booth's detail and promote your employee easily. |
| 6 | `clickAlphaButton` | Click 'hide buildings' on topright:<br><br>To hide other objects except the visitors. |
| 7 | `icon_destroy` | Use 'quick destroy' as the faster way to destroy tiles, plants, or your building. |
| 8 | `exploreObject` | All your created objects have an effect to your resort, they can increase your popularity.<br>Explore the game to increase your research points.<br>It will affect to your Resort's Stars. |
| 9 | `connectTile` | Always connect your booth with walkable tiles, so visitors can find a way to your booth. |
| 10 | `shortcut` | Other Shortcuts:<br>ESC: Finish/Cancel create a new object<br><br>V: Select arrow(mouse)<br>H: hand symbol(drag)<br>Q: Hide/Show buildings |
| 11 | `upgradeBooth` | Upgrade your booth to increase the revenue.<br>Look at the booth experience for the requirement. |
| 12 | `clickVisitor` | Click on visitors to find out what they want and their favourite.<br>But you can easily find a visitor or their booth from 'visitors tab' on tab, then click the spesific object. |
| 13 | `visitor1` | Each visitor has its current cash and stay longer (if booked a room). <br>Make them satisfied and comfort inside your resort, their mood will affect your popularity. <br>Trash and bad service can make them dissapointed. |
| 14 | `visitorOld` | Old visitors come sometimes shows up in the evening.<br>They realy need a Medical Center to check their health. |
| 15 | `specialVisitor` | Special Visitors bring a lot of money and stay longer (if booked a room).<br>But they only visit when certain conditions are met. |
| 16 | `upgradeBooth` | Upgrade your booth to increase the revenue.<br>Look at the booth experience for the requirement. |
| 17 | `createJanitorRoom` | Create Janitor's Room near the garbages, because it has specific cleaning area (janitor's work area).<br>You can expand it by upgrading the booth. |
| 18 | `upgrade_janitorRoom` | By upgrading the Janitor's Room, you will get larger cleaning area.<br>You can hire more janitors. |
| 19 | `janitor1` | Don't forget to hire more janitor to clean the garbages.<br>Janitor gain experience during their work time. Promoting them will increase their performances (and regain stamina), but their sallaries will be increased too. |
| 20 | `extraUpgrade` | RESORT UPGRADE is expensive but can help you make a better resort.<br>Purchase them wisely, so you can unlock more booth. |
| 21 | `expandResort` | EXPAND your resort, to get more area to build new objects.<br>You can expand your resort twice. |
